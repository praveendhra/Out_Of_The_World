ENDLESSDRIVE

Infinite procedural roads · RWD / AWD / FWD physics · Left & right-hand drive cockpits · Traffic & score challenges
W/↑ throttle · S/↓ brake & reverse · AD/←→ steer · Space handbrake · E/Shift shift up · Q/Ctrl shift down · C camera · V look back · drag mouse to look around · H headlights · U hide HUD · T TCS · B ABS · G auto/manual · R reset car · M mute · Esc/P pause
Gamepad: RT throttle · LT brake · left stick steer · B handbrake · RB/LB shift · Y camera · X reset · right stick look · Start pause. Steering wheels: use Controller / Wheel to map axes.
Score: drive fast (distance points) · near-miss traffic (oncoming = double) to build combo up to x10 · drift (great in RWD) · hit speed traps · reach checkpoints before the timer runs out.
Tilt steering: hold your phone sideways like a steering wheel and turn it. Tap CENTER to set straight-ahead. Hold GAS and BRAKE (brake also reverses), HANDBRAKE to drift. Drag the screen to look around. Prefer buttons? Pick Touch steering → Buttons above or tap TILT in-game.
